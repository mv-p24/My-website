*Last updated: October 10, 2026*

**email-pipeline** is a personal automation tool built and used only by Matt Varkony. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains how it handles Google account data.

## What the app accesses

With the account owner's permission, the app uses Google's APIs to:

- **Read Gmail messages** in specific labels (for example, news newsletters and job alerts), so it can summarize them.
- **Label and archive** those messages after they are processed. It never deletes email.
- **Send email to the account owner**, such as a weekly news summary.
- **Write rows to Google Sheets** that the owner created, such as a job-search tracker.

## How data is used

- Email content is processed on the owner's own computer.
- To create summaries, the text of selected emails is sent to Anthropic's Claude API. Anthropic processes it under its [commercial terms](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms) and does not use API data to train its models.
- Summaries and extracted results are stored on the owner's computer and in the owner's own Google Sheets.

## What the app does not do

- It does not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to anyone, apart from the summarization step above.
- It does not use Google user data for advertising.
- It does not access any Google account other than the owner's.

## Storage and security

Google sign-in tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain on the owner's computer. Access can be revoked at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
